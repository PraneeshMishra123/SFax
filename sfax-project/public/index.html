About SFAX

Some things are too personal for a group chat or a screenshot. SFAX lets you write to someone close to you and have it printed and sealed at the post office nearest them, for their eyes only.

The post office only ever sees your name. The message itself never appears on a screen there. It goes straight to the printer and into an envelope with the recipient's name on it.

Download the SFAX Print app

Messages are encrypted. They can only be opened and printed with this app, on the computer connected to your dot matrix printer.

  1. Download and install the app (Windows).
  2. Open it and login with your post office PIN and password.
  3. It creates your secret key on that computer. After that you can print.
